Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 June 2026

Checkers (“the app”) is designed to respect your privacy.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, profile name, chosen avatar, game history, rating and statistics are stored only on your device (in local storage and the app’s own files). This information is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is removed if you delete the app.

Network use

The app works fully offline. The only network communication is with Apple’s App Store when you make an optional in-app purchase (take-backs or hints). Those purchases are processed entirely by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we never see or receive your payment details.

Children

The app does not collect any data and is suitable for all ages.
