Frequently asked

Which checkers variants are supported?

American/English draughts (8×8), International draughts (10×10), Russian draughts, and Turkish draughts. Each has a built-in “how to play” guide.

Does the app work offline?

Yes — Checkers is fully offline. No account, no internet connection required to play.

What are take-backs and hints?

Take-backs let you undo a move; hints point out a strong move. Both are optional in-app purchases, sold as consumable packs. Every game is free to play without them.

I bought a pack but didn’t receive it.

Make sure you’re connected to the internet so the purchase can complete, then reopen the app. If it still hasn’t arrived, email us with the date of purchase and we’ll sort it out.
